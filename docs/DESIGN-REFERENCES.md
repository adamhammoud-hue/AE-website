# AE Alpine visual direction

Updated 15 October 2026. The existing site content, business details and approved Alpine footage remain the source of truth. No reference site's copy, images or proprietary font files were imported.

## References reviewed

- [Savee](https://savee.com/): quiet dark presentation and rounded, compact controls. Public homepage and marketplace reviewed; no private boards accessed.
- [Fonts In Use — Neue Montreal](https://fontsinuse.com/typefaces/106391/neue-montreal) and [Ogg Display](https://fontsinuse.com/typefaces/31926/ogg-display): contrast between restrained sans-serif lettering and more expressive display forms. These commercial fonts are references, not embedded assets.
- [Minimal Gallery — Agency](https://minimal.gallery/tag/agency/): varied project scale, generous spacing and stronger separation between sections.
- [Site of Sites — Shaped Masks](https://www.siteofsites.co/collections/shaped-masks): curved silhouettes and geometry as compositional devices.
- [Site of Sites — Hover Animations](https://www.siteofsites.co/collections/hover-animations): deliberate, responsive interaction.

## Implemented typography

- [Instrument Sans](https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans): main headings, navigation and body copy.
- [Instrument Serif](https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif): italic display accents.
- IBM Plex Mono: small metadata and section labels.

Font files are served locally. Font licenses are included in `assets/fonts/*-OFL.txt`. The Google Fonts Latin and Latin Extended files retain support for the site's French text. No font subscription or generation credits were used.

## Layout and interaction

The opening retains its original full-height proportions and spacious framing, with a small upper-left headline and translucent action. The approved video is unchanged, and supporting text stays below it. Alant and AZUR share aligned image frames and unboxed descriptions. Services use three equally sized, open displays with working enquiry links. The film and studio use precise rectangular image frames. Packages form a single collection, process steps share one axis, and FAQ answers use simple expanding rows.

Service hover effects retain the approved movement of the decorative objects, with subtle pointer response and a growing underline. Project images respond with a small zoom and pointer displacement; package focus and hover states use a restrained colour wash. Text links, arrows and form fields have consistent feedback. Pointer updates are limited to one per animation frame, require a fine mouse pointer, and reset on pointer exit or preference changes. Native detail controls use cancellable height animation. Reduced-motion preferences disable movement and show a static hero image.

## Colour below the opening

Gold accents begin below the hero. Service illustrations and package surfaces follow three distinct tones: champagne, slate blue with gold, and bronze. Project accents complement their existing imagery. Warm details continue through the film, process, studio, FAQ and contact form. The hero and header retain their original blue and silver colours; no geometry or video asset changed. Desktop comparison confirmed an identical opening screenshot and unchanged section dimensions. Package text contrast exceeds 4.5:1 in all three palettes.

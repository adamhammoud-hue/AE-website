THE IMMERSIVE LUXURY APPROACH

For brands that want their website to feel like an experience in its own right. Immersive Luxury brings bespoke art direction, advanced development and richly crafted visuals together, creating a cinematic world around your business, products or services.

AZUR is a concept demonstrating one expression of Immersive Luxury: an atmospheric coastal scene with chairs that move as you scroll. It is a creative reference, not a fixed formula. Your experience could take an entirely different visual and interactive direction.

Discuss this package

The direction we can develop

Bespoke creative direction
A complete visual atmosphere: composition, typography, colour, lighting and pacing, all designed to work together.
Advanced graphics & imagery
A direction for detailed graphics, photographic imagery or cinematic visual scenes. We agree which assets to create, source or refine, and the production involved, as part of your quote.
Custom development & interaction
More ambitious visual behaviour and carefully built interactions. Options can include animated product presentations, immersive transitions or scroll-led scenes where they suit your project.
Luxury in the whole experience
The finish extends beyond the opening: page transitions, mobile framing, readable content and a clear route to contacting you. Scroll animation is optional; the distinctive experience is the focus.

The essentials, in every package. Every website package includes mobile-friendly design, clear service and contact sections, page titles and descriptions for search, launch checks and a website handover.

06 / BEFORE WE START

A few useful
answers.

Have something else in mind? Tell us about your project and we’ll discuss what fits.

What should I prepare before contacting you?

A short description of your business, what you would like to achieve and any timing you have in mind is enough to start. Include your current website if you have one. We’ll discuss the text, images and other material needed when we define the project.

Who do you work with?

Independent professionals and small businesses, including wellness practitioners and local service providers. Our Alant Nutrition project is one example of this approach.

How much will my project cost?

Our website ranges are CHF 800–1,300 for Essential, CHF 1,300–2,500 for Signature, and CHF 2,500–5,000 for Immersive Luxury. Your final quote depends on the pages, content, visuals and functionality you need. The scope, revision allowance and any recurring costs are agreed before work begins.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. Send us the link and tell us what you would like to improve. We’ll review your current setup and discuss a suitable approach.

How long does a project take?

We agree a delivery schedule after reviewing the scope and the content available. Your quote also explains what we need from you and when you can review the work.

Do you offer help after launch?

Monthly support can cover an agreed amount of website updates and promotional content. We define the allowance and fee with you so you know what is included.

Does advertising include managing paid campaigns?

Our advertising service focuses on creative work such as promotional videos and social media visuals. Any campaign management or advertising budget would need to be discussed and agreed separately.

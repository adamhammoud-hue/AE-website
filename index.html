06 / BEFORE WE START

A few useful
answers.

Have something else in mind? Tell us about your project and we’ll discuss what fits.

Who do you work with?

Independent professionals and small businesses, including wellness practitioners and local service providers. Our Alant Nutrition project is one example of this approach.

How much will my project cost?

We provide a tailored quote based on the pages, content, visuals and functionality you need. You’ll receive the scope, revision allowance and any recurring costs before agreeing to start.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. Send us the link and tell us what you would like to improve. We’ll review your current setup and discuss a suitable approach.

How long does a project take?

We agree a delivery schedule after reviewing the scope and the content available. Your quote also explains what we need from you and when you can review the work.

Do you offer help after launch?

Monthly support can cover an agreed amount of website updates and promotional content. We define the allowance and fee with you so you know what is included.

Does advertising include managing paid campaigns?

Our advertising service focuses on creative work such as promotional videos and social media visuals. Any campaign management or advertising budget would need to be discussed and agreed separately.
